## Sanitization standard

Before publication, evidence is reviewed for personal email addresses, tenant domains, subscription and tenant identifiers, workspace identifiers, billing information, credentials, secrets, and unrelated browser or desktop information.

Resource names and private lab IP addresses may remain when necessary to explain the authorized topology and when they do not expose credentials or third-party systems.

## Future captures

Each screenshot should prove a distinct implementation or validation result, use a chronological filename, and be referenced by the lab README. Duplicate or purely navigational screenshots should be excluded.
